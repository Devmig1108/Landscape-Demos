Ways to enter

All qualifying entry methods are equal.

Each eligible individual may receive no more than one entry into the drawing, regardless of entry method or number of qualifying actions completed. No more than two entries may be counted per household.

Qualifying action entry

Complete one qualifying action with Invicta Roofing.

During the Entry Period, any eligible individual who completes at least one qualifying action with Invicta Roofing qualifies for one entry into the drawing.

  • Free roof inspection performed by Invicta
  • Insurance claim filed with Invicta's assistance
  • Maintenance service completed
  • Repair service completed
  • Roof coating service completed
  • Roof replacement completed

The prize

El sueño de la noche could become real.

One prize will be awarded: a quinceañera salon/venue package at an El Paso venue selected by Invicta Roofing, with a stated retail value of $12,000.

The winner selects the event date between February 15, 2027 and December 15, 2027, subject to venue availability and the venue's own terms, availability, and cancellation policies.

Important prize details
  • Prize is non-transferable.

  • Sponsor may substitute a comparable or greater value prize at Sponsor's sole discretion.
  • Winner is responsible for applicable federal, state, and local taxes.

Eligibility

Open only to legal residents of Texas who are 18 years of age or older at the time of entry. Void outside Texas and wherever prohibited by law.

Not eligible

Employees, owners, immediate family members, and members of the same household of Invicta Roofing are not eligible to win.

Winner selection

The winner will be selected by random drawing from all eligible entries on January 8, 2027 at 509 Giles Rd, El Paso, TX 79915.

Odds

Odds of winning depend on the total number of eligible entries received during the Entry Period.

Notification

Winner will be notified by phone and/or email within 5–7 business days of the drawing and may be required to return required documents within 7 days.

Taxes

Because the prize's stated retail value exceeds $600, Sponsor will issue an IRS Form 1099-MISC or successor form to the winner for the value of the prize.

Giveaway FAQ

Quick answers.

No purchase necessary. Full Official Rules are available at invictaroofs.com and in the linked PDF on this page.

Does a purchase or paid service increase my chances?

No. A purchase, inspection, claim, or paid service will not increase your chances of winning beyond the entry structure described in the Official Rules.

Can I complete multiple actions to get multiple entries?

No. Completing additional qualifying actions, or more than one of the same action, does not earn additional entries. The limit is one entry per eligible individual, subject to the two-entry household limit.

Can I enter without doing business with Invicta?

Yes. Eligible Texas residents may enter without any paid service, inspection, or transaction by using the free mail-in postcard method described above and in the Official Rules.

Where will the drawing happen?

The random drawing will occur on January 8, 2027 at Invicta Roofing, 509 Giles Rd, El Paso, TX 79915, and will be broadcast live on Facebook, Instagram, and/or TikTok.

Who sponsors the Giveaway?

The Sponsor is Invicta Roofing LLC, located at 509 Giles Rd, El Paso, TX 79915. Sponsor may be reached at support@invictaroofs.com or 915-630-1349.
